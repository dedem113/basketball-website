Basketball Players

Meet the legends and superstars of basketball

Michael Jordan

Era: 1984-2003

Widely considered the greatest basketball player of all time. Michael Jordan won 6 NBA championships with the Chicago Bulls and 5 MVP awards.

LeBron James

Era: 2003-Present

A four-time NBA champion and one of the most versatile players ever. LeBron plays for the Los Angeles Lakers.

Kobe Bryant

Era: 1996-2016

The "Black Mamba" won 5 NBA championships with the Los Angeles Lakers and was a 18-time NBA All-Star.

Stephen Curry

Era: 2009-Present

Known for revolutionizing three-point shooting. Curry has led the Golden State Warriors to multiple championships as a 2-time MVP.
