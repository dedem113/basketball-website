Basketball Rules

Learn the basic rules of the game

🏃 Game Objective

Score points by shooting the basketball through the opponent's hoop. The team with the most points at the end wins the game.

👥 Team Composition

Each team has 5 players on the court. The game is typically 48 minutes long, divided into 4 quarters of 12 minutes each.

⏱️ Shot Clock

In the NBA, a team must attempt a shot within 24 seconds. If they don't, the ball is turned over to the opposing team.

🚫 Fouls

Physical contact with an opponent that impedes play is a foul. A player who commits 6 fouls in an NBA game is disqualified.

🏃 Traveling

A player can take up to two steps while holding the ball. Taking more than two steps without dribbling is traveling and results in a turnover.

📊 Scoring

Field goals are worth 2 points, three-pointers are worth 3 points, and free throws are worth 1 point each.
